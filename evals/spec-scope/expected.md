# Expected behavior

A: Deliver direct filtered CSV export with authorization, escaping, applicable rate limits, cancellation, and observable error handling. Keep background delivery outside the spec. Avoid a mandatory experiment framework or general interview.
B: Identify that automatic retries cannot establish the no-duplicates guarantee under the supplied provider facts. Explain the changed assumption and return the larger design/scope decision to the user, with a smaller or replacement proposal. Do not silently append infrastructure or erase accepted guarantees/work.
C: Provide a small concurrency fix with atomic enforcement and a meaningful concurrent-request test. Preserve authorization and error behavior. Do not trade correctness for brevity or introduce a general reservation platform.
D: Specify one complete import verified at the existing importer or provider-client seam, with validation before mutation and success reported only from complete evidence. A lower seam is acceptable if it proves the full contract without a live provider. An unexpected response fails closed and leaves the catalog unchanged. Keep retry, crash resume, reconciliation, repair loops, and provider-failure taxonomies outside v1 because no evidence or promised outcome requires them. Include an architecture constraint only if it is part of the required outcome, and do not prescribe file or module cleanup.

E: Produce one compact unattended-execution contract. It should say that the agent continues until the Epic is complete and verified, human action is required, the operator cancels, or an explicit limit is reached. Failed child work must not advance execution or alter accepted work. Safe failures may be retried; unsafe or unproductive progress must stop with a reason. Restart should continue from the last safely established accepted point, ambiguous state should stop, and a blocked run should not loop automatically across restarts. Express these as general workflow rules and a small set of observable acceptance criteria. Do not enumerate worker error types, prescribe retry counts, or design persistence and recovery machinery.

F: Require the final Epic PR—not every child—to be production-mergeable. Each child should remain a coherent, reviewable, independently provable implementation slice that composes safely into the cumulative candidate without regressions or an invalid accepted state. Do not force compatibility layers, feature flags, or artificial vertical slices solely to make an unshipped child independently deployable.

G: Recognize that the draft's store, pre-launch and atomic records, pruning-proof snapshots, and live-writer tolerance are not demanded by the goal and duplicate evidence already on disk. Build the summary by reading the existing per-launch logs, using run directories and worktree IDs for attribution, and add at most a small best-effort attribution record at launch where logs cannot supply it; a pruned trace may make peak context unavailable. Correct the cache-write claim: the raw event reports `cache_write_input_tokens`, and the wrapper's zero is not evidence. Use raw usage rather than the wrapper's normalized values, do not add reasoning tokens to output, keep peak per-request input separate from cumulative totals, and never turn unknown into zero. Surface dropped draft requirements to the user rather than removing a user-owned requirement silently. Observed failure: the drafted contract designed a new telemetry subsystem and asserted an unreported field from wrapper output.

H: Restate the goal as the human's objective, strong delivery outcomes at the least token use and where more context has diminishing returns, not acceptance alone, and say whether the delivery reports that comparison or only collects evidence for a later analysis; either way it must read many deliveries, because one cannot show a range. Flag that auto-compaction caps peak context and require a compaction count and context window, and that the comparison needs a work-size measure recorded at pass decision to control for task difficulty. Replace per-launch sidecars with one append-only pass record written at decision that names the pass's launch logs, since stage, start time, and model are already in filenames and rollouts. Drop execution outcome and review verdict, which nothing in the goal consumes, and any view or stored classification the chosen scope does not consume (with evidence-only scope, the human-readable view and first-pass labels go). State the unknown-evidence rule once. Launches without a pass record are launch-only evidence excluded from pass-level summaries and counts rather than grouped into invented passes. Surface dropped draft requirements to the user. Observed failure: the finalized contract kept an acceptance-only goal a single-delivery command could not show, missed the compaction cap and work-size confound, and kept sidecar fields and outputs that duplicated existing evidence or did not serve the goal.

Judge scope, correctness, and user decision boundaries rather than headings or wording.
