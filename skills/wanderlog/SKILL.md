5. **A pick presented as decided** without Jian's explicit yes? A dinner pick is not a decision until he approves it.
6. **Day notes reference MASTER CONFIRMED BOOKINGS, never duplicate it.** One source of truth; remove doubled wording.
7. **Whitespace or run-together text** in notes? Clean it without touching content, order, times, prices or statuses.
8. **Right date and right segment?** Solo-day edits (Nov 17–20) don't belong on family days, and the reverse. Ginza Kagari is a SOLO dinner.
