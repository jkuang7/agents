# Wanderlog house style and itinerary principles

Learned from a read-only pass over the finished Japan trip. Match this when editing or building any trip; copy the trip's own format if it differs.

## Format

- **Notes** are one text block, uppercase plain-text labels, inline `•` bullets, in this order: BOOKING STATUS KEY, MASTER CONFIRMED BOOKINGS, the default-status rule ("unless a dated note says CONFIRMED, treat the stop as WALK-IN or NOT BOOKED").
- **Master line:** `<date>, <time> — <place>, <N> people: CONFIRMED, PAY ONSITE.` Add what matters on the day: seat limit, arrival time, price per person, fees paid, reference.
- **Day heading:** Wanderlog's full date ("Sunday, November 22nd"). Group days carry `DAY N` (family segment: Nov 21 is DAY 1); solo days start `SOLO —`.
- **Day opening note:** one paragraph, `BOOKING STATUS — ` then each reservation as `✅ <time> <place> for <N> is CONFIRMED, <payment> (<reference>)`, then `All other food/stops are 🚶 WALK-IN.`, then `ROUTE — A → B → C` (parentheses mark optional stops), then any hard rule ("Do not cross back to Asakusa after reaching Skytree."). A day with no bookings says so: `BOOKING STATUS — no confirmed reservations. All stops ... are WALK-IN.`
- **Booking identifiers are written out in the day's BOOKING STATUS line** (the master list is the source; the day line must match it exactly). Don't repeat them in the day's other blocks.
- **Supporting blocks** (separate notes between rules, titled with the day): `WHAT THESE STOPS ARE KNOWN FOR`, `CURATED FOOD + CHILL PLAN` or `CURATED FOOD + BOOKING STATUS`, `ARCHITECTURE ALONG THE ROUTE`, `ACTIVE EXPLORATION TARGET` with `HONEST ESTIMATE:`, `DWELL-TIME SUM:`, `NOT COUNTED:`. Short days use `FOOD —`, `OPTIONAL —`, `PACE —`.
- **Stop cards:** numbered marker, name, category tags, photo, then either a short custom note or Wanderlog's "From the web" text. Status goes in the note, never in the name. Most stops have no scheduled time; fixed ones carry `Scheduled: 12:00 PM - 1:30 PM` plus a note like `12:00 PM–1:30 PM FIXED, CONFIRMED + PREPAID, 2 people, order <number>.`. Optional or walk-in notes read `WALK-IN / OPTIONAL — <where it fits in the route>. Drop it if <condition>.` Travel time and distance between cards are Wanderlog's own estimates; never type them in.
- **Reading tip:** accessibility text truncates long notes at about 500 characters, so read the rest from a screenshot.

## Principles Jian plans by

1. **Anchors first, flexible stops fit around them.** Protect the arrival time before a held table (arrive 15 minutes early).
2. **Finish a district before moving on.** Write the no-return rule into the day note.
3. **Bound every add-on.** Architecture or sights along the route get a dwell time (10–15 minutes) and "no detour".
4. **Seating and recovery are planned, not hoped for.** Name the rest (90 minutes at the hotel, a 20–30 minute seated reset) and condition standing-only or tight venues on everyone fitting.
5. **The group moves together.** If someone wants to stop, everyone returns and eats nearby.
6. **Meals fit the pickiest eater.** Rank options with menu breadth, table seating, capacity and price. Eat early (4:30–5:30 PM) and give a trigger to switch plans ("if arrival slips past 11:30, use <backup>").
7. **Keep proposal, target and booking apart.** `DINNER PICK: ⏳ <place> at 5:30 PM, NOT BOOKED.` / "treat 5:45 PM as a target, not a reservation." / `DINNER — ⏳ UNDECIDED.`
8. **Backups have a role and a trigger.** Say "only the seated backup" rather than scheduling every alternative.
9. **Make risk actionable:** cash only, no seating, line moves fast, sells out, arrive early or accept a queue.
10. **Optional replaces, never extends.** A sample-making stop replaces browsing; at most one more block fits.
11. **No filler.** Write "the day is full without X" and name what was left out on purpose.
12. **Report active exploration apart from the elapsed day:** an honest range, the dwell-time sum, and what it excludes (meals, transit, queues, cafés, rests). Note slopes and uneven ground.
13. **Arrival and departure logistics outrank sightseeing and food.** Arrival night is taxi, snack, sleep; departure puts breakfast after baggage drop and coffee after gate confirmation, with no destination meal.
14. **Unresolved logistics stay visibly unresolved:** `⏳ NOT ARRANGED / PENDING VERIFICATION` plus the missing pieces (handoff, delivery guarantee, payment or waybill). Carry essentials through transfers and plan large luggage separately.
15. **Short cards, long only where operations need it:** most cards get none or 20–40 words; meeting points and cancellation terms get full text.

Smoking and mobility are not recorded anywhere in the trip. Apply the non-smoking rule from the skill anyway, and ask before assuming ages or mobility limits.
